FAQ

Common questions.

Does eKRON have a presale?
No. eKRON and all KRON Family memecoins follow the KRON Fair Launch Standard — no presale and no private sale, ever. Infrastructure tokens (ENK, SOVRA, ENM) may use structured raises; memecoins do not.
What is the KRON Family?
Seven independent experimental memecoins sharing a brand universe: eKRON, soviKRON, mineKRON, alviKRON, puriKRON, puppyKRON, and warpKRON. Each has its own contract and risk profile. See the full roster.
Is eKRON the same as EnteleKron?
No. eKRON is a memecoin. EnteleKron is sovereign infrastructure. Names are connected by brand family — they are not the same asset or protocol.
What is the tokenomics?
Every KRON memecoin: 10B fixed supply, 80% fair-launch LP, 10% ecosystem (12-month lock), 5% founder (6-month lock), 5% founder (12-month lock). Enforced by on-chain timelocks via the launch program.
How do I verify the official contract?
Only trust addresses on the official verification page and announcements from official domains. If it is not verified there, assume it is not official.
When will other KRON tokens launch?
Phase II satellites (soviKRON, mineKRON, alviKRON) launch staggered 2–4 months after eKRON is credible. Phase III spin-offs launch only on organic community demand. No timeline guarantees.
What network is eKRON on?
eKRON V2 is deployed on Base (EVM), chain ID 8453. The official contract is verified on BaseScan. Always confirm you are on Base before interacting.
